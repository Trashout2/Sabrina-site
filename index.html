About Sabrina

Trusted support for your
home and lifestyle

"A refined, personal approach to helping your home feel organized, cared for, and beautifully handled."

Sabrina & Co. was created for homeowners, families, and busy professionals who need dependable help managing the details of home and life. From preparing a property for arrival to coordinating trusted vendors, every request is handled with care, discretion, and follow-through.

With a background in client relationships and a natural eye for organization, I bring a polished, hands-on approach to every home I support. My goal is simple: make your life easier, your home feel cared for, and your time feel protected.

I serve busy professionals, families, and second-home residents throughout coastal Orange County. Whether you need help organizing your home, preparing for guests, coordinating repairs, stocking your refrigerator, or managing day-to-day tasks, Sabrina & Co. provides trusted, personalized support so you can focus on what matters most.
